Legal & Responsible Firework Sales

As per the applicable Supreme Court directions and prevailing rules, online sale of firecrackers is not permitted. We value our customers and, at the same time, respect all applicable laws and regulations.

Customers can select their required products through our Estimate Page and submit their enquiry using the Get Estimate option. Our team will contact you by phone to discuss and confirm the order.

Please use our website to prepare your product estimate and submit your enquiry. We will get in touch with you for further order confirmation.

Santhiya Firework Agencies follows applicable legal and statutory requirements for the sale, storage and handling of fireworks.

Our fireworks are handled and stored in accordance with applicable explosive safety rules and licensing requirements.
